# Context

We're making a social media app which is a Human First application, basically in the AI era, humans are not ready to percieve such change that fast and people still appreciate human art. The issue is that most social media platforms are not cluttered by people who are using AI with bad intentions only for profits and are cluttering the artistic spaces of the social internet with effortless AI slop.

Our social media application is acting like a safe haven where we truly appreciate quality and people who do things for love.
So we only allow a handful of people who are verified by us to create content to post on the platform. Basially we give out "creatorship". And all the content is moderated. The application follows a reddit like structure we have dedicated communities for something and creators belonging to that community and then we have moderators to moderate the content, review the work and check for unauthorative content.

So we prefer quality over quantity.
